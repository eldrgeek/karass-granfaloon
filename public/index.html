Karass or Granfaloon?

A question for the table we shared.

Karass

A team of people flung together by something larger than any of them arranged — bound by a real, if invisible, purpose. You don't audition for one. You just find out, later, that you were in it.

Granfaloon

A false karass. A group that feels like a team because of a label — same conference badge, same room, same hashtag — but has no genuine destiny in common at all.

— terms borrowed from Kurt Vonnegut, Cat's Cradle
